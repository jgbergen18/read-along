  1. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi as this PC.

Add a book

Choose the book's .epub and its audiobook (.m4b). Immersive Reader copies them into your library, matches the chapters, syncs every word and makes the phone version. That takes about 45 minutes; you can keep reading meanwhile.

Folder:

Add a book

Choose the book's .readalong file. Your PC makes one for each book, in the book's folder (for example in OneDrive), and the laptop app's ⓘ book info can also download it.

The book is copied onto this device, so it works without the PC and without internet. It takes a few hundred MB.
